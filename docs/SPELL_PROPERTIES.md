# Spell properties

Generated by `tools/gen_spell_properties_doc.py` from the engine's own reflection (`mods/spelldump`): the 115 item templates that make up the spells (crystals, runes, scrolls, orbs, cast items, magic ammo, projectiles and their effects), all property sets and values as shipped. The hand-written sections are the decompiled facts of [`RE.md`](RE.md) section 29 (Script_Game.dll, Script.dll, Game.dll). Edit the numbers with `risen.balance.apply { spells = { ... } }` / `new_spells` (see [`LUA_API.md`](LUA_API.md) "Spells") or, for anything else, `risen.template.patch`.

## How a spell works

There is **no spell object and no spell template**: `gCItem_PS.Spell` and `gCInteraction_PS.Spell` are empty in every shipped template (605 / 598 templates, all `(no template)`). A spell is a group of items and scripts:

* **The item you hold** carries a `gCCastInfo_PS`: `ManaCost`, `ConsumeItem` (scrolls and orbs are used up), the cast item shown in the hand (`CastItem`, `It_Cast_*`), the ammo shown while aiming (`CastAmmo`, `It_MagicAmmo_*`), the item for a failed cast (`CastFail`: `It_MagicAmmo_NoMana`, `It_Cast_*_Fail`), the AI task that casts it (`StartTask`: `PS_MagicBall`, `PS_MagicMissile`, `PS_Cast_Interact`, `PS_Summon_Skeleton`, ...) and the focus mode. The equip condition (`gCItem_PS.RequiredSkills`) is the skill level a character needs: `Fireball` / `Frost` / `Missile` 1 for the crystals, `Circle` 1..4 for the runes (scrolls and orbs need nothing).
* **Projectile spells** (Fireball, Frost, Missile) are cast with a **crystal** (`It_Crystal_Fire`, `It_Crystal_Frost`, `It_Crystal_Magic`; equip slot 11). The engine's shoot script (`OnShootMagicFireball`/`...Frostball`/`...MagicMissile*`) spawns a **projectile template** (`It_Projectile_Fireball_S0`, `It_Projectile_Frost_S0`, `It_Projectile_Missile_S0`: `gCDamage_PS` + `gCProjectile2_PS` + `gCEffect_PS`) and calls `PSCastInfo::Consume` on the equipped item, which takes `ManaCost` from the caster's base mana (and, with `ConsumeItem`, one item of the stack). The projectile template is chosen **per spell type** (the combat move of the spell type: `gCCombatMoveShoot.Projectile`), not per crystal: in a live cast at Fireball skill 1, 2 and 3 the spawned template was always `It_Projectile_Fireball_S0`; `S1`/`S2` exist but were never spawned.
* **Rune spells** (everything else) come as **rune** (permanent, needs a Circle level), **scroll** (used up, needs nothing) and **orb** (free, used up) items of `UseType` `gEItemUseType_Cast` (slot 10). Their effects are scripts: `InventoryUse_Player_HealMinor/Medium/Enhanced`, `...Light`, `...Protection`, `...Berserker`, `...Beast`, `...Levitate`, `...Nautilus` (use scripts that call `PSCastInfo::Consume` themselves) and `PS_Summon_Skeleton/Inferno/Illusion`, `PS_Cast_Interact`, `PS_Cast_Joke` (states of the cast animation).
* **The magic skills** (`gESkill`): `Circle` 21, `Fireball` 22, `Frost` 23, `Missile` 24. An NPC is a "magic user" with `Circle > 0` and `EquipBestMagicWeapon` gives it the crystal of its best of Fireball/Missile/Frost.

## Where the numbers live

| What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| mana cost | `gCCastInfo_PS.ManaCost` of the crystal / rune / scroll / orb (`mana_cost`, `scroll_mana_cost`, `mana_mult` in `spells`) | **verified live**: the cast takes exactly this from the base mana (`PSCastInfo::Consume`); `CanCast` = `ManaCost <= total mana` |
| skill requirement | `gCItem_PS.RequiredSkills` (`requires`) | checked when the **player** equips (`CanEquipHasRequieredStats`); NPCs equip anything; `risen.item.can_use` is the engine check |
| used up by a cast | `gCCastInfo_PS.ConsumeItem` (`consume`) | `PSCastInfo::Consume` removes one item; a stack cast from the **magic slot** keeps its last item (engine quirk, RE.md section 29): `new_spells` scrolls of projectile spells are removed by the layer |
| projectile damage | `gCDamage_PS.DamageAmount` of the projectile template (`damage`, `damage_mult`) | **verified live**: the area-damage hit takes `DamageAmount + 25 * Fireball skill - protection` (Frost: `+ 15 * Frost`; Missile: `+ 15 * Missile`) |
| damage type | `gCDamage_PS.DamageType` (`damage_type`) | `Magic` for Fireball/Missile, `Ice` for Frost: picks the protection skill that is subtracted (`ProtMagic`, `ProtIce`, `ProtFire`, ...) |
| projectile speed / size | `gCProjectile2_PS.StartVelocity`, `SweepTestRadius`, `EnableSweepTest` (`speed`, `radius`) | the Fireball skill also scales the shooting speed (`OnMagicFireBallGetSpeedFactor` = `10 * Fireball`) |
| flying / hit effects | `gCEffect_PS.Effect` of the projectile (`effect`), `gCProjectile2_PS.ShootEffect` | the impact effects (`eff_projectile_fireball_impact_01`, ...) are strings inside the hit scripts |
| what is cast | `gCCastInfo_PS.StartTask`, `CastItem`, `CastAmmo`, `CastFail`, `FocusMode`, `CastInfo` | change with `properties` to **re-use** another spell's effect for an item (a rune with the `StartTask` of another spell), see `new_spells` |
| gold value, icon, name | `gCItem_PS.GoldValue`, `IconImage`, the loc ids `FO_<template>` / `ITEMDESC_<template>` | like any item |

## Who carries spell items

NPC and monster templates with a rune, scroll, crystal or orb in their template inventory: `Skeleton_SitDead_Berengir` (It_Scr_MinorHeal x1). Magic NPCs get their crystal from the script `EquipBestMagicWeapon` (`AssureItems`) when their Fireball/Frost/Missile skill is above 0, so the templates do not list them.
